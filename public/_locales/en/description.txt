Turn your Chrome new tab into a bookmark launcher with a floating Dock, grouped shortcuts and backgrounds you choose.

TWO WAYS TO ORGANIZE
• Dock desktop: flip through pages with the mouse wheel, search your saved bookmarks and drag shortcuts into the bottom Dock.
• Grouped bookmarks: organize shortcuts in collapsible groups, drag them between groups and manage several at once. A shortcut pinned to the Dock can stay in its group.
• Import selected Chrome bookmarks or all of them. Back up your shortcuts and groups as a local JSON file.
• Press Ctrl+Y (Cmd+Y on Mac) to add the current page. Choose a light or dark theme and use English or Simplified Chinese.

YOUR BACKGROUND
Choose a color, image or local video, or import a compatible Wallpaper Engine Scene. In Wallpaper Engine, open the wallpaper’s folder in Explorer. In the extension, go to Settings → Scene wallpaper and choose the folder or select scene.pkg with its optional project.json.

Some Scene effects respond to your mouse. Compatibility varies by scene. System audio effects and Web/HTML wallpapers are unsupported. This extension is independent of Wallpaper Engine.

PRIVACY
Your bookmark collection, layout settings and imported wallpaper files stay on your device. Bookmark and wallpaper features require no account. The extension does not use ads, analytics, tracking or bookmark cloud sync.

Site icon lookups send only a shortcut’s domain to a public icon service. Preset and online backgrounds load from their image hosts. The optional Tasks panel connects to the Xuyan website only after you allow access. It uses your existing website session to read or manage your tasks.
