Make Chrome’s new tab your own with grouped bookmark shortcuts and a customizable background.

• Organize shortcuts into groups and drag them between groups. Move, copy or delete several at once.
• Switch between the floating bottom Dock and grouped cards from the new tab toolbar. Pin a shortcut to the Dock while keeping it in its group.
• Choose a solid color, a preset image, your own image or a local video.
• Import a local Wallpaper Engine Scene folder or select its scene.pkg with the optional project.json. Compatible scenes render in the new tab. SceneScript, audio and Web/HTML wallpapers are unsupported, so some scenes may appear different from Wallpaper Engine.
• Import existing Chrome bookmarks or use Import / Export to back up your shortcuts as JSON. Background media files stay on the device and are separate from the JSON backup.
• Press Ctrl+Y (Cmd+Y on Mac) to add the current page. Choose a light or dark theme, and use English or Simplified Chinese based on your Chrome language or your preference.

No account, ads, analytics or cloud sync. Shortcuts, settings and imported wallpaper files are stored on your device. To display site icons, public favicon services receive the shortcut’s domain, without the rest of its URL. Preset images and image URLs you choose load from their hosts.
