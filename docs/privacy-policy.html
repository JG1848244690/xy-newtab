中文

本地数据

  • 快捷方式的名称、网址、图标、分组以及布局和语言等设置存储在本机。选择导入 Chrome 书签时，扩展读取本机书签并创建快捷方式。
  • 扩展只在你点击工具栏图标或使用快捷键时读取当前标签页的网址和标题，用于预填添加表单。
  • 导入的图片、视频和 Wallpaper Engine Scene（含所选的项目元数据）保存在本机并作为新标签页背景使用。
  • 点击导出时，扩展在本机生成快捷方式和分组的 JSON 文件。备份文件由你管理。

网络请求

  • 查找网站图标时，扩展会向 icon.horse、DuckDuckGo 或 Google 发送快捷方式的域名。请求不包含该快捷方式的网页路径、查询参数、标题或页面内容，也不携带 Cookie。图标会缓存在本机。
  • 预设图片（含默认背景）从 Unsplash 加载。你设置的网络图片从相应的主机加载。这些服务可能收到 IP 地址等常规连接信息。

数据使用与删除

扩展无需账号，不提供云同步，不接入广告、分析、遥测或崩溃上报。开发者不接收你的书签、设置或导入文件，也不出售或出租用户数据。本扩展对从 Google API 获取的数据的使用遵守 Chrome 应用商店用户数据政策，包括「有限使用（Limited Use）」要求。

你可以在扩展里删除快捷方式和分组，或在设置中移除当前 Scene 的本地副本。卸载扩展会清除它在浏览器中的本地存储。

本扩展不面向 13 岁以下儿童，也不会有意收集其个人信息。政策更新时会修改顶部生效日期。

English

Local data

  • Shortcut names, URLs, icons, groups and settings such as layout and language are stored on your device. When you import Chrome bookmarks, the extension reads your local bookmark tree and creates shortcuts.
  • The extension reads the current tab’s URL and title only when you click its toolbar icon or use its keyboard shortcut to prefill the Add form.
  • Imported images, videos and Wallpaper Engine Scenes, including any project metadata you select, stay on your device and are used as new tab backgrounds.
  • Export creates a local JSON file containing shortcuts and groups. You manage that file.

Network requests

  • To look up a site icon, the extension sends the shortcut’s domain to icon.horse, DuckDuckGo or Google. This omits the shortcut’s path, query string, title and page content. Icon requests omit cookies. The icons are cached on your device.
  • Preset images, including the default background, load from Unsplash. If you specify an online image URL, your browser requests that image from its host. These services may receive ordinary connection information such as your IP address.

Data use and deletion

The extension has no account, cloud sync, ads, analytics, telemetry or crash reporting. The developer does not receive your bookmarks, settings or imported files, or sell or rent user data. The extension’s use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

You can delete shortcuts and groups in the extension or remove the local copy of the current Scene in Settings. Uninstalling the extension removes its local browser storage.

The extension is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal information. We update the effective date above when this policy changes.
